Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Flow ("the Game") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Flow mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Flow does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

The Game does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information.

2. Game Data

Flow may store game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress
  • Completed levels
  • Game settings
  • Difficulty preferences
  • Other gameplay state required for the Game to function

This information is used only to provide and maintain the gameplay experience.

3. Internet Access

The core gameplay of Flow does not require personal information to be transmitted to the developer.

If future versions introduce online functionality, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Flow does not use advertising or third-party analytics services that collect personal information, unless explicitly stated in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or similar third-party services are introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what information those services may process.

5. Children's Privacy

Flow is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the Game does not require users to provide personal information, the developer does not knowingly maintain personal information belonging to children.

6. Permissions

Flow only requests device permissions that are necessary for features provided by the Game.

The Game does not intentionally access contacts, messages, photographs, precise location, microphone recordings, or other sensitive personal information unless such access is clearly required by a future feature and disclosed to the user.

7. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

Because Flow does not intentionally collect personal information, the developer does not share such information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

8. Data Security

Game information stored locally on your device is subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

Removing the Game may remove locally stored game data, depending on the device and operating-system backup settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Flow's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will include a revised "Last updated" date.
